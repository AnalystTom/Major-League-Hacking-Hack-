Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026. Resurface turns the links and X bookmarks you save into short skills and videos. This page says what we store, who receives it, how long we keep it and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Your account: the email and password you sign up with (we keep only a salted hash of the password), or your X username if you sign in with X.
  • The links you paste and the X bookmarks you import, with the post text, article text, video transcripts and metadata we extract from them.
  • The skills, scripts, rendered videos, captions, posters and narration audio generated from those saves.
  • If you connect X: encrypted access and refresh tokens. Resurface asks X only for read access to your profile and bookmarks (scopes tweet.read users.read bookmark.read offline.access). It never posts, likes or follows on your behalf.
  • Operational records: a sign-in session cookie, usage counts used to enforce daily and monthly limits, and short-lived sign-up rate-limit records.

Cookies

We set only essential cookies: rs_session keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie protects the X sign-in step. We use no advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, so no cookie banner is needed.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who sees it

Your saves, skills and videos are private to your account unless the Resurface team selects a generated skill or video to appear in the public feed and public skill catalog. To ask us to remove a featured item, email tom@thomasmannai.com from your account email.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, saved links, imported X bookmarks, and generated skills and videos are kept until you delete them or your account. Resurface does not yet delete them automatically.
  • Sign-in sessions stop working 30 days after you sign in.
  • X sign-in attempt records are short-lived and removed during normal operation.
  • Sign-up rate-limit records are short-lived and removed during normal operation.
  • X access and refresh tokens are kept while your X account is connected. Disconnecting X clears them.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app from account settings. Deleting removes your account, your saved links, your choices, the skills and videos made only from your links, and your X tokens. When another person also saved the same public link, its skill and video stay for them, but nothing that ties them to you is kept.

If you cannot use the app, email tom@thomasmannai.com from your account email, or tell us your X username, and we will delete the same data. You can also revoke Resurface's access at any time in your X settings under Connected apps.
