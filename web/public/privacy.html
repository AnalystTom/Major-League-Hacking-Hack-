Resurface

Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026. Resurface turns the links and X bookmarks you save into short skills and videos.

What we store

  • Your account: the email you sign up with, or your X username and display name if you continue with X.
  • The links you paste and the X bookmarks you import, with the text, transcripts and metadata we extract from them.
  • The skills and videos generated from those saves.
  • If you connect X: encrypted access and refresh tokens. Resurface asks X only for read access to your profile and bookmarks (scopes tweet.read users.read bookmark.read offline.access). It never posts, likes or follows on your behalf.

How it is used

Only to run Resurface for you: to import your saves and to generate your skills and videos. Content you save may be sent to AI providers that do the extraction, summarising and video narration. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who sees it

Your saves, skills and videos are private to your account unless you explicitly make an item public.

Deleting your data

Email tom@thomasmannai.com from your account email, or tell us your X username, and we will delete your account, saved content, generated skills and videos, and X tokens. You can also revoke Resurface's access at any time in your X settings under Connected apps.
